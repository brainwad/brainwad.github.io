Tax schedules

Each schedule = optional lump sum at $0 + marginal rates. Edits here and on the graph stay in sync.
Data: Parliamentary Budget Office, Small Model of Representative Taxpayers (SMART) dataset, March 2026 (2025-26 MYEFO update), CC BY-SA 4.0. Estimates apply each schedule to the mean taxable income of 4,970 representative taxpayer groups, with no behavioural response. Change in tax take and surplus/deficit are measured against modelled revenue under current law 2025-26, which is within $0.02B of the dataset's actual net tax paid. Non-residents are approximated by each group's "Unknown or overseas" share. Existing tax credits are the gap between 2025-26 statutory tax (rates, LITO, Medicare levy with singles low-income reduction) and each group's actual net tax. Those credits are always subtracted in the budget figures (floored at $0 per taxpayer). Negative credits, where a group actually paid more than modelled current law, are kept for current law itself but dropped for any scenario that differs from it; the rate and comparison lines on the graph exclude them.
